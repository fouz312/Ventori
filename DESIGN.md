# Vantora design system
* Name: **Vantora** ("your pocket computer"). Logo: a monitor outline (violet) holding a "V" mark (cyan) on a deep-indigo field — original artwork in `res/drawable/ic_logo_foreground.xml`, used for the adaptive launcher icon (with monochrome layer), the splash screen and in-app mark.
* Colours: background `#0E1020`, surface `#171A30`, primary violet `#7C5CFF`, accent cyan `#22D3EE`, status green/amber/red.
* Typography: system sans-serif (Roboto) with a compact scale (Headline 28 bold, Title 20/16 semibold, Body 14).
